About Nibras PDF

Merge, split, compress, rotate, protect, sign, scan, Word, and text. Recent documents stay on the home screen. Your files stay on your device.

Nibras PDF is a mobile app that makes everyday document work simpler. It has 18 tools, and your files are processed mainly on your device.

Nibras PDF features

✨

Merge

Combine several PDFs into one file. Pick the files one after another and save them as a single document.

⚡

Split

Divide a PDF into two parts by page count: in a 25-page file, entering 5 gives pages 1–5 and 6–25. You can keep both parts or only one.

🔒

Compress

Rewrites the PDF to reduce its file size. If the file would not get smaller, the original is kept so you never get a worse result.

🧩

Image to PDF

Turns one or more images into a PDF document. Handy for receipts, notes and photos you need to send as a single file.

🎯

PDF to image

Saves PDF pages as images. Useful when you need to post or send a single page as a picture.

📚

Rotate

Turns the PDF left or right. Every page turns in the direction you choose, then the file is saved.

🖊️

Protect

Locks the PDF with a password (at least 6 characters) using AES-128 encryption. The password is entered twice to avoid typos.

📤

Watermark

Adds your own text, for example “Confidential”, to the document. You choose its position on the page, its transparency and which pages get it.

🗂️

Manage pages

Delete pages, change their order, or insert pages from another PDF. The last change can be undone before you save a new PDF.

🔍

Scan

Uses the camera to turn paper documents into a PDF. The document is cropped automatically and several pages can go into one file.

🖼️

Add signature

Draw your signature with your finger and place it on a PDF page. You can drag it, resize it and add more than one signature.

🛡️

Recognize text (OCR)

Extracts text from images on your device. Choose the language — Azerbaijani, English, Turkish, Russian or Arabic — and copy the result.

✨

Extract text from PDF

Recognizes the text on PDF pages, including scanned ones, directly on your device so you can copy and reuse it.

⚡

PDF to Word

Saves the text of a PDF as a Word (DOCX) file that you can keep editing.

🔒

PDF preview

Look through the pages of a PDF: swipe between pages and pinch with two fingers to zoom.

🧩

Batch processing

Choose several PDFs and apply the same operation to all of them at once: compress, rotate, add a watermark or split into pages.

🎯

Select and extract pages

Choose the pages you need and get them as a new PDF. The original file stays unchanged.

📚

Word to PDF

Converts a Word (DOCX) file into a PDF document ready to share or print.

Privacy: document content is not sent to our servers for PDF, image, OCR or scan processing; OCR and scanning run on the device, and the camera is used only while scanning. The free version opens every tool with daily limits (10 document operations, 4 OCR, 5 scans, files up to 100 MB). Pro removes the daily limits and allows PDFs up to 250 MB.

Who is it for?

For anyone who often works with PDFs and images: students, office workers and small business owners who need to merge, compress or sign documents, scan paper or pull text out of an image.

How to use it

  1. Download Nibras PDF for free from Google Play.
  2. Open the app and pick what you need: Merge, Split, Compress, Image to PDF.
  3. Your data stays on your device; only what you share yourself leaves the app.

Privacy and data

🔒

Overview

Nibras PDF is a local-first mobile app for working with PDF documents and images. This policy explains what information the app uses, when information can leave your device, and how you control your choices. If language versions differ, the Azerbaijani version prevails.

🔒

Your files

PDFs and images you select are processed on your device. Merge, split, compress, rotate, watermark, signature, page tools, Writer, PDF to image/Word (DOCX), scanning and text recognition (OCR) do not send document content to Nibras PDF servers. OCR runs on the device using Google ML Kit (Latin script) and Tesseract (Arabic and Russian/Cyrillic).

🔒

Camera and images

The camera is used only while you scan a document. Access to images is used only to create a PDF or extract text from the images you select. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

🔒

Account (optional)

Sign-in with Google is optional and handled by Clerk, which may process your email address, name and a unique Clerk user ID to run the account and app services. See clerk.com/privacy.

🔒

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Settings › Account › “Delete account”. Your Clerk account is permanently deleted, you are signed out and account-related caches on the device are cleared. Server-side usage-limit records and campaign metadata linked to the account are kept for at most 90 days and then deleted. Files and history on your device are not deleted automatically; you can clear them in Settings or by uninstalling the app. An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled automatically — cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. If you cannot access the app, email nibrascode@gmail.com from your account email; requests are completed within 30 days.

🔒

Usage limits

Free use has daily limits: 10 document operations, 4 OCR and 5 scans. Guest counters are stored only on the device. For signed-in free users, the Clerk user ID, the UTC date and these counters may be stored on our server when it is reachable; no document content, images, PDFs or file names are stored. If the server cannot be reached, the app continues with the on-device limit. If you join the optional Premium campaign, metadata such as the campaign tier and discount percentage may be linked to your Clerk user ID. Daily free-trial counters for Pro features (for example watermark or batch processing) are stored only on the device and are not sent to the server. Pro users have no free limits.

🔒

On-device history and settings

Recent files, Writer drafts, language and appearance, app settings, temporary files and local lock settings (PIN and biometric lock) are stored on your device. You can manage history, drafts and temporary files in Settings. On-device data stays until you delete it, the app clears it automatically, or you uninstall the app.

🔒

Google Drive

Google Drive is not active in version 1.0.5. It is shown as “Coming soon” in Settings and on the save screen. No PDF, image or text is sent to Google Drive in this version, and no Drive permission is requested. If the feature is added in a future update, connecting will be optional and only files you choose may be uploaded to your own Drive with Google’s drive.file permission.
