About مجموع الفتاوى

A reader for Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah's Majmu' al-Fatawa (may Allah have mercy on him), keeping the printed edition's page numbers. The text is in Arabic. No accounts, ads or tracking.

مجموع الفتاوى features

✨

Fully offline: the whole text is inside the app

⚡

Fast search across all volumes, jump to volume/page

🔒

Dark, light and paper reading modes

Who is it for?

For students, researchers and readers of Arabic who study Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah's Majmu' al-Fatawa and need to jump to a volume and page or search all volumes.
