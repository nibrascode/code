Privacy policy — Nibras Erase

This policy covers Nibras Erase, version V 1.0.0, made by Nibras Code. Contact: nibrascode@gmail.com.

What the app does

Nibras Erase removes a photo background, can place a color or a blur, a shadow and a circle crop, and lets you fix the edge by hand. The result is PNG, JPG or WebP.

Where the photo goes

The photo is not uploaded to a Nibras Code server. Background removal runs on your device. The first time, a model file may be downloaded. That download is the model, not your photo.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone, account, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, or payment data. There is no account and no ad tracker. Personal data is not sold.

Permissions

A file picker opens the photo and saves the result. The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: nibrascode@gmail.com. If this policy changes, this page is updated.
