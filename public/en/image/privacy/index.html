Privacy policy — Nibras Image

This policy covers Nibras Image, version V 1.0.0, made by Nibras Code. The project is Mahir Əliyev’s independent work. Contact: nibrascode@gmail.com.

What the app does

Nibras Image compresses a photo on the device, converts JPG, PNG and WebP, and shortens the long side. The photo is not sent to a Nibras Code server and is not sent to a cloud model.

How a photo is handled

The file is read only when you pick it. Processing happens on your device, inside the browser or the app. When you download, the result is written where you choose. We keep no copy. If the system file picker offers the camera, that photo still stays on the device and is not sent to us.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone, account, contacts, precise location, advertising ID, payment data, or health data. There is no account, no ad tracker, no analytics SDK, and no third-party cookie. We do not sell or share personal data, because it does not reach us.

What stays on the device

Only the language you pick is stored on that device. It is not sent to a server. Clearing the app data deletes it.

Permissions

A file picker is used to open a photo and save the result. The app does not ask for the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated. Questions: nibrascode@gmail.com.
