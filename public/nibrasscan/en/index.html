Privacy and data

🔒

Summary

Nibras Scan is a document scanner. Everything you scan is processed and stored on your device. We do not run servers for this app, we do not create accounts and we do not show ads. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

🔒

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect or receive your name, e-mail, location, contacts, device identifiers, analytics data, or the content of your scans. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

🔒

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your device: photos of pages you scan (temporary), the PDF documents you make, and your app settings (default filter, PDF quality, OCR language). This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. You can delete it at any time via Settings → Delete all data or by uninstalling the app.

🔒

Permissions

The camera is used only to photograph the documents you want to scan. Images are not uploaded anywhere. The app itself does not send your data over the internet. The internet permission is declared because Google ML Kit may download its on-device text recognition model through Google Play services the first time it is used.

🔒

Third-party components

Google ML Kit recognises text on your device. Your images are not sent to Google for recognition. Google may process technical information (for example model download and diagnostics) as described in the ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy. Tesseract OCR (open source) recognises Arabic text fully offline on your device.

🔒

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you explicitly share it or open it in another app using Android's share sheet. What that other app does with the file is governed by its own privacy policy.

🔒

Retention and deletion

Your documents stay on your device until you delete them. Use Settings → Delete all data to remove all documents and temporary images. Uninstalling the app removes all app data. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete remotely.

🔒

Security

Your files are kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure. Please protect your device with a screen lock.
