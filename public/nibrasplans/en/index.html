Privacy and data

🔒

Summary

Nibras Plans is a timetable and daily planner app. Everything you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

🔒

Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, device or advertising identifiers, analytics, or the content of your plans. The app contains no ad or analytics SDKs.

🔒

Data stored on your device

Your plans, lessons, tasks, completion status and settings are stored only in the app's local storage (SharedPreferences) and are never sent to a server.

🔒

Free version and Premium

In version 1.0.0 all features are free. No account is required. A paid Premium tier does not exist yet (coming soon); no payment information is collected.

🔒

Notifications and gallery

Lesson reminders are shown only as local notifications on your device. When you save your timetable as a PNG image, it is written to your gallery only at your request; the app does not read your other photos. If you share the image, the receiving app's own policy applies.

🔒

Third parties

No Firebase, Google Ads, Facebook SDK or other tracking services are used. No data is shared with third parties.

🔒

Deletion

You can delete plans inside the app; uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything. Since nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete.
